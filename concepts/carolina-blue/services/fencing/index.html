Vinyl Fence & Gate Installation

Low-maintenance vinyl fences and gates that add privacy, keep pets and kids safe, and look good for years.

A good fence turns a backyard into a private, usable space. We install vinyl fences and gates that never need painting or staining, will not rot or splinter, and clean up with a garden hose.

We help you plan the whole job: where the fence line runs, the height and style that fit your home and HOA, and gates sized for how you actually use the yard, from a walk gate to a wider gate for mowers and trailers.

Fencing in Triangle neighborhoods

Most neighborhoods in Apex, Cary and Holly Springs have HOA rules for fence height, style and color, and towns can have their own requirements too. Vinyl's clean, consistent look tends to fit those standards well, but always get approval before work starts.

Fences usually sit just inside the property line, so it helps to have your survey or plat handy. Before any post holes are dug, underground utility lines need to be marked through NC 811.

Frequently asked questions

Why choose vinyl fencing?

Vinyl does not rot, rust or need painting or staining, and it cleans up with a hose. It is a popular low-maintenance choice for privacy and backyard fences.

Do I need HOA approval for a fence?

If you live in an HOA neighborhood, almost certainly. Many HOAs set rules for fence height, style, color and placement, and your town may have requirements too.

Where should my fence go?

Fences usually sit just inside the property line. Your survey or plat shows where the line is, and many HOAs also have rules about placement.
