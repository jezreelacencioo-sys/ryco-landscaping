Sod Installation in Apex, NC

Go from bare dirt or worn-out turf to a thick, green lawn in a single day.

Sod is the fastest way to a finished lawn. Instead of waiting months for seed to fill in, you get full, green grass the same day, and it holds the soil in place right away on slopes and bare areas that keep washing out.

What makes sod last is the prep underneath it. We clear out the old turf and weeds, loosen and grade the soil, add topsoil where it is needed, and lay the sod tight with staggered seams so it knits together and roots down instead of drying out at the edges.

Sod for Triangle lawns

Most new construction in Apex, Holly Springs and Cary is built on compacted red clay with very little topsoil left behind. Sod laid straight on that clay often struggles, so we prep the ground first to give the roots something to grow into.

Tall fescue sod does best installed in fall or early spring when temperatures are mild. Warm-season grasses like Bermuda and zoysia are installed from late spring through summer, when they are actively growing.

Frequently asked questions

How soon can I walk on new sod?

Keep foot traffic light for the first two weeks while the roots grow down into the soil. After that, normal use is fine. Wait until it has rooted before the first mow.

How should I water new sod?

Water deeply right after installation, then keep the sod and the soil underneath it moist for the first couple of weeks. Once it has rooted, switch to deeper, less frequent watering.

Is sod better than seed?

Sod gives you an instant lawn and controls erosion right away, which is great for new construction and slopes. Seed costs less but takes much longer to fill in. We can help you decide based on your yard and budget.
