Christmas Light Installation

A full holiday lighting package: we bring the lights, hang them, keep them glowing all season, then take them down and store them for next year.

Overview

Hanging Christmas lights on a two-story roof in the cold is nobody's favorite job, and buying, testing and storing lights every year adds up. With our seasonal package there's nothing for you to buy or store. We supply all the lights and materials and install a custom design made for your home.

If anything goes out during the season, we come back and fix it. After the holidays we take everything down on the dates set for your home and store the lights until next year.

Book early

Many homes in Apex, Cary and Holly Springs are two stories with steep peaks and several rooflines. They look fantastic outlined in lights, and they are exactly the kind of ladder work that is better left to a crew.

Schedules fill up as the holidays get closer, so October and early November are the best times to book. Lights usually go up in November and come down in early January.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to buy the lights?

No. We supply all the lights and materials, install them, and store them for you between seasons.

What does the seasonal package include?

Your custom design, the lights and materials, installation, repairs during the season, takedown after the holidays and storage until next year.

What if a section goes out?

Just let us know and we'll come back and fix it.

When do the lights come down?

After the holidays, usually in early January, on the dates set for your home.

Can you install lights I already own?

Yes. We also do one-time installs with your own lights. Just ask for a quote.
