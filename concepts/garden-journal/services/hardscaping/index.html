Patios, Walkways & Fire Pits

Outdoor living spaces built on a solid base, so they look great on day one and for years after.

Overview

A patio, walkway or fire pit can completely change how you use your yard. What makes it last is everything you never see: a properly excavated and compacted base, and a plan for where the water goes.

We build paver patios and walkways, extend driveways with pavers, install pavers right over existing concrete, build fire pits, and lay gravel and stone paths, including permeable options that help with runoff.

Built for clay and rain

Red clay holds water and moves as it gets wet and dries out. Combined with heavy summer downpours, that is hard on anything built on top of it. Pavers laid on a thin or poorly compacted base settle and shift.

A properly compacted gravel base, the right slope away from the house, and permeable options where runoff is a problem are what keep a hardscape level and solid for the long run. If you are in an HOA, plan on getting patio approval before work starts.

Questions

Good to know.

Can you install pavers over my existing concrete?

Often, yes. If the concrete is in reasonably good shape, pavers can be installed over it for a whole new look without tearing it out. We check the slab during your free estimate.

What are permeable pavers?

Permeable pavers have gaps filled with gravel and sit on an open stone base, so rainwater soaks through instead of running off. They can help with drainage and puddling.

How long does a paver patio last?

Built on a properly excavated and compacted base, a paver patio can last for decades. Individual pavers can be lifted and reset if needed, which makes repairs much easier than with poured concrete.
