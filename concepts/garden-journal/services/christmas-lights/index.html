Christmas Light Installation

A beautiful holiday display without climbing a single ladder. We design it, hang it, and take it down.

Overview

Hanging Christmas lights on a two-story roof in the cold is nobody's favorite job. We handle it from start to finish: a custom design and mockup, professional installation of commercial-grade lights, and takedown and storage when the season is over.

We light rooflines and peaks, wrap trees and bushes, and light up columns and entryways. Already have your own lights? We also do one-time installations using your materials.

Get a free estimate
Book early

Many newer homes in the Triangle are two stories with steep peaks and multiple rooflines. They look fantastic outlined in lights, and they are exactly the kind of ladder work that is better left to a crew.

Installation schedules fill up as the holidays get closer, so October and early November are the best times to book. Lights are typically installed in November, ready to turn on whenever you like.

Questions

Good to know.

When should I book Christmas light installation?

As early as you can. October and early November are the best times to book, since schedules fill up as the holidays get closer.

Can you install lights I already own?

Yes. We offer one-time installations using customer-owned lights and materials.

Do you take the lights down?

Yes. Takedown and storage are available, so your lights come down safely after the holidays and are ready to go next season.
