Mascot concepts

Rough drafts to pick a direction. Each wears a Carolina Premier cap in your colors. Once you choose one (or mix ideas), a professional illustrator can turn it into polished artwork for the site, trucks and shirts.

Concept 1

Rocky the Cardinal

NC's state bird. Instantly "Carolina," friendly and bold. Red pops against your green.

Concept 2

Rusty the Fox

Clever and quick. Reads as "sharp work." Easy to draw in action poses (mowing, trimming).

Concept 3

Shelby the Turtle

NC's state reptile, with a shell that looks like a freshly striped lawn. Unique and memorable.

Concept 4

Acorn the Squirrel

NC's state mammal, always busy in the yard. Holding an acorn, it ties right into tree services.

Concept 5

Mo the Mower

A friendly mower with a face. Says "lawn care" at a glance and works great on trucks and yard signs.

Concept 6

Big Jer the Bear

Black bears live in NC. Strong and dependable, a good fit for tree work and heavy jobs.
