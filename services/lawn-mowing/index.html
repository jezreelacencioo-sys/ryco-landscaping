Local know-how

Mowing for Triangle lawns

Most lawns in Apex and the surrounding towns are tall fescue, a cool-season grass that stays healthiest when it is mowed tall, around 3 to 4 inches, especially in July and August. Cutting fescue short in summer is one of the most common reasons it thins out and lets crabgrass take over.

Bermuda and zoysia lawns in full sun are a different story. They do best kept much shorter and grow fastest in the heat. Whichever you have, we cut it the way that grass wants to be cut.

FAQ

Lawn Mowing & Maintenance FAQ

How often should my lawn be mowed?

During peak growing season most lawns look best mowed every week. In slower stretches, like the heat of late summer for fescue, every other week can be enough. We can set a schedule that fits your yard and budget.

What height should tall fescue be mowed in summer?

Keep tall fescue on the high side, around 3 to 4 inches, during the summer. Taller grass shades the soil, holds moisture and crowds out weeds. Avoid removing more than about a third of the blade in a single cut.

Should grass clippings be bagged?

Usually not. Clippings left on the lawn break down quickly and return nutrients to the soil. Bagging mostly makes sense when the grass has gotten very tall or before an event when you want a spotless look.
