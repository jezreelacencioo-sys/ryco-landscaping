Local know-how

Built for clay and rain

Red clay holds water and moves as it gets wet and dries out. Combined with heavy summer downpours, that is hard on anything built on top of it. Pavers laid on a thin or poorly compacted base settle and shift, and retaining walls without drainage behind them can bulge or fail.

A properly compacted base under patios and walkways, and gravel backfill with drainage behind walls, is what keeps a hardscape level and solid for the long run.

FAQ

Hardscaping FAQ

How long does a paver patio last?

Built on a properly excavated and compacted base, a paver patio can last for decades. Individual pavers can also be lifted and reset if needed, which makes repairs much easier than with poured concrete.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes. Water building up behind a wall pushes against it and is a leading cause of wall failure. Gravel backfill and a drain line behind the wall let water escape instead of building pressure.

Can you do the landscaping around a new patio too?

Yes. We handle lawn, beds, mulch and pine straw too, so the area around your new patio or walkway can be finished at the same time.
