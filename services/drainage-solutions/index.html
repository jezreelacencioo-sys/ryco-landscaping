Local know-how

Why the Triangle floods

Red clay barely absorbs water. When a summer thunderstorm or tropical system drops inches of rain in an hour, the water has nowhere to go but across the surface, into low spots and toward your foundation.

New construction makes it worse: lots are graded quickly, topsoil is stripped, and downspouts often dump right next to the house. Small fixes like extending downspouts and correcting the grade can make a big difference.

FAQ

Drainage Solutions FAQ

What is a French drain?

A French drain is a trench filled with gravel around a perforated pipe. Water soaks into the gravel, flows into the pipe and is carried away to a safe spot, keeping the yard and foundation dry.

Why does water pool near my foundation?

Usually because the ground slopes toward the house, or downspouts empty right next to it. Regrading and buried downspout extensions are often the fix.

Can drainage fix my dead grass?

Often, yes. Grass dies in areas that stay soggy for days after rain. Fixing the drainage and then repairing the lawn gives it a chance to grow back healthy.
