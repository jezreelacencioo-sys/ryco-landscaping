Hardscaping in Fuquay-Varina: FAQ

Are gravel paths a good option for large yards?

Yes. Gravel and stone pathways cost less, drain well and look natural, which makes them a great fit for longer runs across bigger properties.

Can you build a fire pit away from the house?

Yes. On bigger lots, a fire pit out in the yard with a gravel or paver path leading to it makes a great gathering spot.
