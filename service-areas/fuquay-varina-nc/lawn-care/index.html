Lawn Care & Maintenance in Fuquay-Varina

Serving Fuquay-Varina homes

Fuquay-Varina properties tend to have more yard than homes closer to Raleigh, and keeping up with a big lawn takes real time every week. We handle the mowing, edging, string trimming and blowing on a weekly or bi-weekly schedule so your weekends are yours again.

Larger lots also mean larger problem areas: bare patches, washouts and thin turf along wooded edges. We repair them with soil prep, topsoil and seed or sod, and keep the whole lawn thick with fall aeration, overseeding and fertilization. In autumn, we clear the leaves that pile up from surrounding trees.

How do I fix washed-out areas in my lawn?

Washouts usually need the soil rebuilt with topsoil, then seed or sod, and sometimes grading to slow the water down. Fixing the cause keeps it from washing out again.
