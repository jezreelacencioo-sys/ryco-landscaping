FAQ

Landscaping in Holly Springs: FAQ

How fast do privacy trees fill in?

It depends on the tree. Fast growers like Green Giant arborvitae can add a couple of feet a year once established, while hollies grow more slowly but stay dense. Planting larger trees gives you more privacy right away.

Can you fix low spots and poor drainage in my yard?

Yes. We grade and level uneven areas so water drains away from your home, and use river rock or stone where water naturally runs.
