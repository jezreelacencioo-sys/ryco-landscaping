Local challenges

What Holly Springs yards deal with

Young lawns that have not filled in

Sod or seed on new construction often struggles in the first few years. Aeration, overseeding and mowing at the right height help it thicken up.

Bare beds on new homes

New homes often come with thin landscaping. Fresh mulch or pine straw and clean edges make a big difference fast.

Drainage on clay lots

Graded clay lots can shed water fast. Healthy turf and well-built hardscapes help manage runoff.

Holly Springs FAQ

My new lawn is thin. What should I do?

Fall core aeration and overseeding is the most effective fix for thin fescue lawns. Mowing high and watering deeply but less often also helps roots grow deeper.

Yes. We serve neighborhoods across Holly Springs, both new and established.
