Raleigh FAQ

Which parts of Raleigh do you serve?

We mainly serve southwest Raleigh, the side of the city closest to our Apex base. If you are elsewhere in Raleigh, call or text and we will let you know if we can help.

Can grass grow under big oak trees?

It can be tough. Shade-tolerant fescue blends, fall overseeding, mowing high and thinning tree canopies to let in more light all help.
