FAQ

Hardscaping in Cary: FAQ

Can you build a patio near big tree roots?

Often, yes, but it takes planning. We design around major roots where possible and may suggest a different spot or a gravel or permeable option to protect the tree.

Is a paver patio better than concrete?

Pavers handle soil movement better because each piece can shift slightly without cracking, and damaged pavers can be lifted and replaced. Concrete is usually cheaper up front but cracks and is harder to repair.
