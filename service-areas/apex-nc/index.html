Local challenges

What Apex yards deal with

Thin lawns on new construction

Many newer Apex homes have only a thin layer of topsoil over hard clay. Fall aeration and overseeding are the best way to build a thicker lawn over time.

Summer heat on fescue

Tall fescue is the most common lawn here and it struggles in July and August. Mowing high through summer keeps it from thinning out.

HOA standards

Many Apex neighborhoods have HOA rules for lawn height, edging and beds. Regular maintenance keeps you clear of violation letters.
