FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call, text or send the form on our contact page with your address and what you need, and we will get back to you with a price.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Apex, NC and serve Cary, Holly Springs, Fuquay-Varina, Morrisville and Raleigh. Not sure if you are in range? Just ask.

When is the best time to aerate and overseed in Apex?

Most Triangle lawns are tall fescue, a cool-season grass. Early fall, roughly September through October, is the best window because soil is still warm and the weather is cooling down.

Do you handle both lawn care and tree work?

Yes. From weekly mowing to tree trimming and removal, one call covers everything outside your home.
